Buying shares opens or increases a position. Selling reduces an existing position and
returns the trade proceeds to your available balance after the fill settles.

## Before you trade

- Read the market rules and resolution source.
- Check the best bid, best ask, spread, and available size.
- Confirm that your balance covers the order and estimated fee.

## Buy shares

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Choose an outcome

    Open the market and select the outcome you expect to win.

  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Build the order

    Choose Market or Limit, enter the amount or number of shares, and review the
    estimated price, total, and fee.

  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Sign and monitor

    Submit the order. Filled shares appear in your portfolio; unfilled Limit quantity
    appears under Open Orders.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Sell shares

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Select Sell

    Open the market, choose the outcome you hold, and switch the order panel to **Sell**.

  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Choose execution

    Use Market for immediate execution against bids, or Limit to set your minimum price.

  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Review and sign

    Confirm the shares, estimated proceeds, and fee. Monitor open quantity in
    **Portfolio → Open Orders**.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
  You cannot sell more shares than your available position. Shares committed to an open sell order may be unavailable
  until that order fills or is canceled.
</Callout>

You can sell before resolution or hold a winning position through settlement and claim
its payout.

## Split and merge shares

Open the **Merge / Split** menu beside Buy and Sell in the order panel.

- **Split:** convert 1 USDC into one Yes share and one No share for the selected market.
- **Merge:** combine one Yes share and one No share back into 1 USDC.

Enter the amount, review the available balance or paired shares, and confirm the wallet
request. These actions convert collateral and complete pairs directly; they do not place
an order on the book.

## How complementary orders settle

In a two-outcome market, both outcome tokens belong to the same condition and form one
fully collateralized pair. The CLOB can therefore settle compatible orders in three ways:

| Matching orders                               | Settlement                                                                                                                                               |
| --------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `BUY` and `SELL` for the same outcome         | Transfers shares to the buyer and collateral to the seller.                                                                                              |
| `BUY` for outcome A and `BUY` for outcome B   | **MINT:** splits $1 of collateral into a complete pair and sends each buyer the selected outcome. The limits cross when their prices sum to at least $1. |
| `SELL` for outcome A and `SELL` for outcome B | **MERGE:** combines a complete pair back into $1 of collateral and pays both sellers. The limits cross when their prices sum to at most $1.              |

For example, `BUY A @ $0.15` can match `BUY B @ $0.85` without waiting for a
seller. Complementary sells at `$0.15` and `$0.85` can likewise settle by merging
the paired shares. Only the common executable quantity fills; any permitted remainder
stays open with its existing priority.

Your activity still shows the physical order you submitted. A `SELL A` remains a
`SELL A`, even when the exchange settles it together with a complementary sell through
a merge.
